Help us reproduce an issue quickly

When applicable, include:

  • FlexDoc version and the package or adapter you are using
  • framework/runtime and browser version when relevant
  • minimal reproduction steps plus expected and actual behavior
  • a small OpenAPI example or screenshot when it is safe to share
  • logs or errors with tokens, credentials and private data removed

Security or private data

Do not put credentials, private OpenAPI documents, customer data, or security-sensitive details in a public issue. Email contact@prauga.com instead.
